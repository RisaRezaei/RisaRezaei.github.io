      Method

      Same code as my GSW Lake Explorer: lake outlines from HydroLAKES v1.0; yearly water classes from JRC Global Surface Water v1.4 (Landsat, 1984–2021). For each lake, permanent water (water in every observed month of a year) and seasonal water (water in only some of those months) are summed inside its outline plus a buffer of 50·√A metres (at least 300 m; A = lake area in km²), at a pixel size adapted to lake size: 30 m for lakes under 225 km², coarser for larger ones.

      Years in which more than 5% of this area had no Landsat observation are left out; each lake needs at least 10 such years and a mean permanent water area of at least 0.05 km².

      Trends: least-squares linear regression, significant at p < 0.05, given per decade: permanent water as % of the lake's mean permanent area, seasonal and total water as % of its mean total area. Every trend is cross-checked with the Mann–Kendall test and Sen's slope. Abrupt shifts: Pettitt change-point test on total water (p < 0.05, at least 3 years on each side).

      Behaviour follows the signs of the significant permanent, seasonal and total trends. Lakes with none are a "Step change" if a shift of 10% or more is significant, "Pulsing" if total water varies by 20% or more between years (coefficient of variation) or over 30% of it is seasonal, and otherwise "Stable".

      Lake facts (type, depth, volume, residence time, watershed) come from HydroLAKES. Marker size scales with mean total water area.
