02 — ABOUT

Water, satellites and data.

I'm an environmental engineer (MSc, Sharif University of Technology) working at the interface of hydrology, satellite remote sensing and machine learning.

I build Earth-observation workflows in Google Earth Engine and Python, model surface water and groundwater with WEAP, MODFLOW and MIKE, and turn model results into decision support for water management.

My decision-support work for the Lake Urmia basin, carried out with the University of Delaware, is published in the Journal of Hydrology and was presented at AGU24. I also have hands-on experience in water-quality laboratory analysis and review regularly for Elsevier journals.
