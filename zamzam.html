Inside the Zamzam Tehran wastewater plant

For my final project in Industrial Wastewater and Effluent Treatment at Sharif University of Technology I had to design a treatment tank. To check my assumptions against a working system, I arranged a visit to the on-site treatment plant of Zamzam Tehran, a soft-drink producer.     What I took back to my design

    Load, not just flow

    Beverage effluent is dilute in volume but heavy in dissolved sugar, so the tank had to be sized on organic load (BOD) and aeration demand, not on hydraulics alone.

    Foam and nutrients

    The foam sprays and the phosphorus stock on site showed that sugar-rich wastewater needs foam control and nutrient balancing for healthy biomass.

    Settling decides success

    The clarifier only works if the sludge settles. The daily SV30 check made it clear that sludge settleability belongs in the design, not just in operation.
