      Method (same code as my GSW Lake Explorer): lake outlines from HydroLAKES v1.0; yearly water classes from JRC Global Surface Water v1.4 (Landsat, 1984–2021). For each lake, permanent water area is summed inside its outline plus a buffer of 50·√A metres (at least 300 m; A = lake area in km²), at a pixel size adapted to lake size: 30 m for lakes under 225 km², coarser for larger ones. Years in which more than 5% of this area had no Landsat observation are left out; each lake needs at least 10 such years and a mean permanent water area of at least 0.05 km². Trend: least-squares linear regression of yearly permanent water area, given as % of the lake's mean area per decade and called significant at p < 0.05. Marker size scales with mean permanent water area.
